## Interview Preparation Tips

### Technical Interview:
1. **Explain Your Thinking**: Talk through your thought process
2. **Ask Clarifying Questions**: Don't make assumptions
3. **Start Simple**: Begin with basic solution, then optimize
4. **Consider Edge Cases**: Think about error handling
5. **Write Clean Code**: Follow the principles you learned

### Behavioral Interview:
1. **Prepare STAR Stories**: Situation, Task, Action, Result
2. **Show Passion**: Demonstrate enthusiasm for learning
3. **Be Honest**: Admit what you don't know, but show willingness to learn
4. **Team Collaboration**: Share examples of teamwork
5. **Problem-Solving**: Explain how you approach challenges

### Questions to Ask Them:
- What does a typical project look like?
- How does the team collaborate?
- What technologies do you work with most?
- How do you approach code quality and reviews?
- What opportunities for learning and growth exist?
- What's the deployment process like?
- How do you handle technical debt?
